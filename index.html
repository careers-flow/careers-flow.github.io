Frequently asked questions

Is Careers Flow really free for candidates?⌄

Yes. We never charge candidates and never ask for payment, deposits or "processing fees". If anyone asks you for money in our name, please report it to us.

Who will see my resume?⌄

Only Careers Flow and the employers whose requirements you appear to match. We tell you before forwarding your profile, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

How long until I hear back?⌄

Does applying guarantee an interview or job?⌄

No. Shortlisting depends on how closely your profile matches the employer's criteria, and the employer alone decides on interviews and hiring.

How do I delete my data?⌄

Email us from the address you applied with and we will delete your profile. See our Privacy Policy for details.
